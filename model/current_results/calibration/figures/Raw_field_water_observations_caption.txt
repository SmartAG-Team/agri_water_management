Published field observations for winter wheat and summer maize, 2016–2019. ET is inferred from the 0–2 m soil-water balance; initial storage and depletion are source measurements. Lines connect annual treatment means and do not denote continuous monitoring. Source ET dispersion is unavailable. The independently reconstructed 2016 maize rainfall is used, and all ET observations remain included. Maize W0–W3 labels denote preceding wheat irrigation.
