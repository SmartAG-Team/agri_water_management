Cumulative simulated actual evapotranspiration and published seasonal water-balance ET totals at Wuqiao. Colored lines are model trajectories; colored endpoint markers are observed seasonal totals. The field dataset does not contain observed daily ET trajectories. Wheat treatment labels describe current irrigation, whereas maize labels describe the preceding wheat treatment; current maize management is shared within each year. The 2016–2018 seasons enter calibration and 2019 is retrospective testing. Actual ET comprises transpiration, soil evaporation and canopy evaporation. Observed ET assumes negligible runoff and drainage; numerical replicate dispersion is unavailable.
