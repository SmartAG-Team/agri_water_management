Figure S4. Original Yucheng harvest observations at YCAZH01ABC_01. Panels (a, b) show wheat aboveground biomass and grain; panels (c, d) show maize aboveground biomass and grain. Faint points are six quadrat observations; dark markers and error bars are means ± replicate standard error. Magenta diamonds identify the identical full maize source vectors in 2012 and 2017. Raw values remain displayed for source inspection; both groups are excluded from comparisons with quality-controlled observations. Error bars describe sampling variation, rather than model or policy uncertainty.
