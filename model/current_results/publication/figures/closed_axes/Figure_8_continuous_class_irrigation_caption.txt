Figure 9. Continuous annual irrigation strategies during 2014–2025. (a) Field irrigation; (b) grain retained relative to conventional irrigation; (c) crop-plus-fallow ET reduction; (d) annual production–irrigation combinations. Blue circles and orange squares identify 95% and 98% selection targets. Shading and open markers identify missing-GRACE years with conventional quotas. Grain and ET can still differ in those years because preceding strategy decisions alter carried soil-water states. Matched rainfall-only histories coincide with the storage–rainfall histories.
